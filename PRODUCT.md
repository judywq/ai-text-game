# GenQuest

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Language learners who want a more memorable, story-led way to practise reading, vocabulary, and decision-making. They use GenQuest on desktop and mobile for short, self-directed learning sessions.

## Product Purpose

GenQuest turns language practice into interactive narrative: readers choose how a story progresses and return to vocabulary through review quizzes. Success means a learner feels drawn back into the next page rather than faced with a conventional exercise.

## Positioning

Every choice writes the next page: adaptive language learning presented as a living storybook.

## Brand Personality

Inviting, literary, and quietly adventurous. The interface should feel like opening a well-made illustrated book while remaining clear and dependable as a learning product.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic gamified-learning dashboards, flat template cards, and image-based text. Do not let live controls sit axis-aligned over angled book artwork; live content must follow the page perspective.

## Design Principles

- Make reading and choice-making the central experience.
- Treat illustration as a setting for usable, accessible HTML—not as a replacement for it.
- Keep the book illusion precise: HTML writing planes follow the page frame's calibrated perspective.
- Preserve a clear path into a story, account, and vocabulary review.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls and vector text, maintain readable contrast, support keyboard interaction, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
